Take to a doctor

Ferritin before any iron. Haemoglobin is normal at 13.5, and a haemoglobin variant may explain the borderline red-cell indices on its own. Iron you don't need is not harmless with a fatty liver.

Your data

Everything you enter is written straight to this phone and stays there. No account, no server, no subscription — it works with the phone in aeroplane mode. The one thing it can't survive on its own is the phone itself, which is what the backup file is for.

  1. Every day: nothing to do. Sets and ticks save the moment you enter them.
  2. Once a month: tap Save a backup file. It lands in Downloads — move one copy off the phone, to Drive or an email to yourself, whatever you already use.
  3. New phone, or after a mishap: open the app there, tap Restore, pick the JSON file. Everything comes back, merged by date so nothing overwrites anything newer.

Your numbers

These live in your data, never in the app's code, which is why the published files carry nothing personal. Leave the target blank and it sits at 7% below your start — the point at which the evidence says liver fat clears.

Offline & install

Once installed, the whole app lives on the phone — it opens with no connection at all, which is the point, because gym basements have no signal. New versions only arrive when you open it with a connection.
